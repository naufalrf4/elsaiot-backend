# Product Context: ElsaIoT Backend

## Purpose and Problem Statement

ElsaIoT is designed to address the challenge of continuous water quality monitoring in various environments (aquaculture, wastewater treatment, public water systems, etc.). Traditional water quality monitoring involves manual sampling and lab testing, which is:

- Time-consuming and labor-intensive
- Provides only periodic snapshots of water quality
- Delays detection of critical issues
- Makes it difficult to identify patterns and trends over time

ElsaIoT solves these problems by providing:

- Continuous, real-time monitoring of water quality parameters
- Automated data collection and analysis
- Early detection of water quality issues
- Historical data analysis for trend identification

## Core User Personas

1. **Water Quality Engineers**

   - Need: Real-time monitoring of water quality parameters
   - Goal: Ensure water quality meets required standards
   - Pain points: Manual sample collection, delayed test results

2. **Facility Managers**

   - Need: Overview of water quality across multiple locations
   - Goal: Efficient resource allocation and problem prevention
   - Pain points: Lack of centralized monitoring, reactive management

3. **Technical Operators**

   - Need: Device management and calibration capabilities
   - Goal: Maintain reliable sensor network
   - Pain points: Complex calibration procedures, device maintenance

4. **Data Analysts**
   - Need: Access to historical water quality data
   - Goal: Identify trends and patterns for optimization
   - Pain points: Fragmented data sources, manual data processing

## User Experience Goals

1. **Intuitive Monitoring Interface**

   - Real-time dashboards showing key water quality parameters
   - Clear visualization of parameter thresholds and alerts
   - Historical data views with filtering and comparison capabilities

2. **Streamlined Device Management**

   - Simple device pairing and registration process
   - Clear device status indicators and health monitoring
   - Guided calibration workflow with step-by-step instructions

3. **Proactive Alerting System**

   - Customizable alert thresholds for water quality parameters
   - Multi-channel notifications (in-app, email, SMS)
   - Alert acknowledgment and resolution tracking

4. **Comprehensive Reporting**
   - Automated report generation for compliance purposes
   - Customizable reporting periods and parameters
   - Export capabilities in multiple formats

## Key Value Propositions

1. **Reduced Operational Costs**

   - Minimize manual water sampling and testing
   - Early detection of issues prevents costly remediation
   - Optimize chemical usage through precise monitoring

2. **Improved Water Quality**

   - Continuous monitoring enables rapid response to issues
   - Data-driven optimization of treatment processes
   - Consistent compliance with quality standards

3. **Enhanced Decision Making**

   - Access to real-time and historical data
   - Identification of patterns and trends
   - Data-driven approach to water management

4. **Scalable Monitoring Infrastructure**
   - Start with key parameters and expand as needed
   - Deploy across multiple locations with centralized management
   - Flexible configuration to adapt to different monitoring needs
